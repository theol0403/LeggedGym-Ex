\section{Hyperparameters}
\label{sec:hyperparameters}

Tables~\ref{tab:ppo_params}--\ref{tab:reward} list the PPO and DAgger
training hyperparameters, PD control and domain randomization
parameters, and reward terms used in all experiments unless noted
otherwise.  The per-step reward is
$r_t = \sum_i w_i \, r_i(s_t, a_t)$ with unitless scales $w_i$.

\end{table}
