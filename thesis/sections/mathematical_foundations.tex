\subsection{Problem Formulation}

We model the parkour task as a Partially Observable Markov Decision
Process $(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{O}, T, R, \gamma)$
with state space $\mathcal{S}$, action space $\mathcal{A}$,
observation space $\mathcal{O}$, transition dynamics $T$, reward
function $R$, and discount factor $\gamma$.
The agent receives partial observation $o_t \in \mathcal{O}$
and selects action $a_t \in \mathcal{A} = \R^{12}$ (target joint
position offsets).  Observations decompose into three groups:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Proprioception} $o_\text{prop} \in \R^{53}$: base
    angular velocity, projected gravity, velocity and heading
    commands, joint positions, joint velocities, previous
    actions, and foot contacts.
  \item \textbf{Exteroception} $o_\text{ext}$: scandot
    heightmaps ($\R^{132}$) for the teacher, or depth/RGB images
    for the student.
  \item \textbf{Privileged} $o_\text{priv}$: friction and other physics parameters available only during teacher
    training.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{PPO}
\label{sec:ppo}

The teacher policy $\pi_\theta$ is trained with Proximal Policy
Optimization (PPO)~\cite{schulman2017proximal}, which maximizes a
clipped surrogate objective:
\begin{equation}
  L^{\text{CLIP}} = \E_t\!\left[\min\!\left(
      r_t \hat{A}_t,\;
      \clip(r_t, 1{-}\epsilon, 1{+}\epsilon)\,\hat{A}_t
  \right)\right],
  \label{eq:ppo}
\end{equation}
where $r_t = \pi_\theta(a_t|o_t) / \pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(a_t|o_t)$
is the probability ratio and $\hat{A}_t$ is the
GAE~\cite{schulman2016high} advantage estimate.

\subsection{DAgger Distillation}
\label{sec:dagger}

The student policy is trained via Dataset Aggregation
(DAgger)~\cite{ross2011reduction}.  At each step the student observes
$o_\text{prop}$ and a depth image and produces $a_\text{student}$,
but the teacher's action $a_\text{teacher}$ is executed.  The student
minimizes:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} =
  \underbrace{\|a_\text{teacher} - a_\text{student}\|_2}_{\text{action loss}}
  + \underbrace{\|y_\text{oracle} - \hat{y}\|_2}_{\text{yaw loss}},
  \label{eq:dagger_loss}
\end{equation}
where $\hat{y} \in \R^2$ is the student's predicted heading and
$y_\text{oracle}$ is the ground-truth heading.
